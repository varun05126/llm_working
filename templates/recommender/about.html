About SkillHer

AI-powered skill assessment and personalized learning paths designed for women in technology and leadership.

Our Mission

SkillHer bridges the confidence and opportunity gap by providing objective skill benchmarking, realistic gap analyses, and actionable weekly roadmaps tailored to individual schedules and goals.

How It Works

  1. 1. Dynamic Skill Assessment: Specify any domain or specialized skill to generate adaptive concept and practical scenario evaluations.
  2. 2. Realistic Gap Identification: Pinpoint exact topic strengths and missing fundamentals scored deterministically on the server.
  3. 3. Actionable Weekly Roadmap: Receive structured 4-week or 12-week milestones with project ideas, free docs, vetted YouTube topics, and entry internship opportunities.
