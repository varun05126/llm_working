Our Mission

About SkillHer

AI-powered skill assessment and personalized learning paths designed for women in technology and leadership.

Closing the Opportunity Gap

SkillHer bridges the confidence and opportunity gap by providing objective skill benchmarking, realistic gap analyses, and actionable weekly roadmaps tailored to individual schedules and goals. Every roadmap blends core computer science fundamentals, hands-on portfolio projects, curated open-source resources, and active community networks for women in tech.

How It Works

Step 1

Dynamic Assessment

Specify any domain or specialized skill to generate adaptive concept and practical scenario evaluations tailored to your level.

Step 2

Deterministic Analysis

Pinpoint exact topic strengths and missing fundamentals scored deterministically on the server with constructive feedback.

Step 3

Actionable Roadmap

Receive structured 4-week or 12-week milestones with project ideas, free docs, vetted YouTube topics, and entry internship opportunities.
